# Gashamna Spatial Analysis

Spatial analysis of archaeological finds, fauna distribution, topography, and historical text-mining for the **Gåshamna** site (Hornsund, Svalbard), expanded into a multi-site comparative framework across Svalbard.

## Notebooks & Data
- `GAS_Hex_Density.ipynb`: Hexagonal grid binning for bone density distribution.
- `GAS_DBSCAN.ipynb`: Density-based spatial clustering of artifact and fauna finds.
- `GAS_Anatomic.ipynb`: Spatial distribution of anatomical species categories (Whale, Walrus, Polar Bear, etc.).
- `GAS_Bone_Size.ipynb`: Fragmentation and spatial bone size distribution analysis.
- `GAS_Topo.ipynb`: Elevation, slope, terrain, and shoreline integration.
- `1994_survey.geojson`: Survey data of 6 Blubber Ovens & Trapper Hut.
